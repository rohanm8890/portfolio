Temperature-Controlled Incubator

A small temperature-controlled enclosure built to maintain a stable warm environment using a microcontroller, temperature feedback, relay-controlled heating, and active airflow.

Goal
Create a low-cost enclosure that could regulate and hold an elevated internal temperature.
Result
Reached and maintained approximately 80–85°F during testing.

What I built

I designed and assembled the prototype around a micro:bit-based control system. Temperature readings were used to control resistive heating elements through relays, while a fan helped circulate air through the enclosure for more even heating.

How it works

The controller continuously checks temperature and switches the heating system on or off depending on the measured value. The enclosure and airflow were iterated on during testing to improve heat retention and reduce temperature variation.

Technologies

micro:bit JavaScript Relay Control Temperature Sensing Resistive Heating Fan Control Prototyping

What I learned

The project gave me hands-on experience connecting software logic to real hardware, debugging sensor and wiring behavior, and iterating on a physical system based on test results.
